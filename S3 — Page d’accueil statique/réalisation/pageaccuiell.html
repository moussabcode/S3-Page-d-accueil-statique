What is this?

An online magazine that brings together several vendor shops—each with its own dedicated space—which can also function as an individual website when accessing a specific shop’s area. Visitors can browse and make purchases online, or simply get a preview before heading to the marketplace.

What does it achieve?

A website that connects business owners, market traders, craftspeople, or merchants—via online platforms—with buyers;

More options

  • The site is divided into several sections:
    -Product sections (comprehensive and randomized);
    -Categories such as clothing, electronics, etc.;
    -A section for stores (available on the site).
  • A security system between the seller and the buyer to prevent fraud and scams.
  • Store owners can specify their exact geographic location, while visitors have the option to search within a defined area.
  • An adaptive interface with customizable appearance settings.
  • AI-powered assistance for users.
  • Communication channels for users (buyer-to-seller, administrator-to-seller, and buyer-to-administrator).
  • Intelligent system to attract users (free account creation, free start for service providers…).
